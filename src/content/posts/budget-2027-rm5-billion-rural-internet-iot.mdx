Most conversations about technology in farms, plantations and small plants start with the clever part: the sensor, the camera, the dashboard. The unglamorous part is the one that decides whether it works: a connection that stays up.

## What the budget says

Bernama reports that Budget 2027 sets aside **RM5 billion** to boost internet connectivity, following the National Digital Network Plan (JENDELA), with a focus on rural and remote areas, including Orang Asli villages. The same report notes RM130 million for the creative industry.

## Why this matters on the ground

- A soil sensor or a water-level node only helps if its readings reach someone.
- A camera that watches the forest edge at night is only useful if the alert arrives in seconds.
- A manager in Kuala Lumpur can only see what the supervisor sees if both sides are online.

Better coverage does not replace good design. We still plan systems to keep working when the signal drops, by storing readings on the device and sending them later, but stronger networks make remote monitoring cheaper and more reliable for more places.

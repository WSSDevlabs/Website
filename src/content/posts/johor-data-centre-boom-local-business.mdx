Johor has become one of the biggest data centre markets in the region. Knight Frank's Data Centre Atlas 2026 puts Johor's incoming pipeline at **8,542MW**, the largest in Asia Pacific, with co-location vacancy of just **0.7%**. It values the market at **US$39.11 billion (RM159.9 billion)**, second in Asia Pacific after Japan.

Nationally, Malaysia approved **RM34.6 billion** in data centre and cloud investments across 33 projects in the first quarter of 2026 alone. [BMI counts](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/10/09/bmis-malaysia-data-centre-overview/) 125 facilities with 925.6MW of live capacity, 2GW under construction and another 3.1GW planned.


## Power is the constraint

Every analyst points to the same pressure: electricity and water. BMI notes that new tariffs for facilities above 100MW could raise their energy costs by 10% to 14%. The Johor government has set up a special technical committee to assess new data centres on electricity demand, water use and infrastructure readiness.

## What it means if you are not a data centre

**Energy will stay on the agenda.** Grid upgrades, tariff changes and demand from large users all push businesses to understand their own consumption. Firms that meter by machine and cut waste will feel price changes less.

**Skilled people are in demand.** Electricians, technicians, network and IoT engineers are being pulled toward large projects. Training your own staff, and using monitoring to reduce site visits, protects you from that squeeze.

**Local infrastructure gets better.** More capacity nearby means lower latency for cloud services and more hosting options in Malaysia, which matters for data you'd rather keep in the country. Budget 2027 also mentions plans for a sovereign AI cloud.

**There is work around the edges.** Every new facility needs security, monitoring, maintenance and facility services. Local firms that can show reliable digital processes are better placed to win that work.

## A practical next step

Start with your own energy. Clamp-on meters on your biggest loads, a dashboard your manager checks and alerts when consumption spikes will tell you more in a month than a year of bills.

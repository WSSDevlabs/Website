Visit Malaysia 2026 is in its final quarter. Arrivals in the first half of the year reached **21.2 million**, according to figures shared at the MATTA Fair in September, and recent reports put the full-year goal at **47 million international arrivals**. Earlier in the year, the Tourism Ministry said arrivals through February were up more than 30% on 2025.

Budget 2027 also extends the tourism tax relief of up to RM1,000 for attraction and cultural entrance fees, so domestic travel gets a nudge too.

## Where the bookings go

Most visitors decide where to stay, eat and visit on a phone, in a few minutes, often in a language other than Malay or English. They pick businesses they can find on Google Maps, check reviews for and book without a phone call.

If your hotel, homestay, tour or restaurant fails any of those steps, the booking goes to the next listing.

## A checklist for the last quarter of VM2026

- **Google Business Profile:** correct hours, photos from this year, replies to reviews.
- **A fast mobile website** with prices, location and a booking or WhatsApp button above the fold.
- **Payment that tourists use:** cards and DuitNow QR at minimum.
- **Key pages in the languages your guests speak.** Look at your reviews to see which ones.
- **Instant replies:** a WhatsApp Business catalogue or a chatbot for the questions you answer every day.
- **For homestays:** smart locks and a door camera make late check-ins easier for you and your guests.

## What to do if you only have a week

Fix the Google profile first, then the website's first screen on a phone. Those two changes reach the most visitors for the least money.

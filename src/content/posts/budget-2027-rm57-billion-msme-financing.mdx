If you run a small business, a national budget can feel far away. This year it is worth a closer look, because several measures land directly on SMEs.

## The numbers, as reported

- **RM57 billion** in loan and financing guarantees for micro, small and medium enterprises, a record amount, according to Malay Mail.
- **Lower SME tax rates.** Berita Harian reports 14% on the first RM150,000 of income and 16% on the band from RM150,000 to RM600,000.
- **RM30 million from MDEC** to help **4,000 MSMEs** adopt AI and automate their operations.

## What this means for a small business

Cheaper tax and easier financing do not fix a slow week by themselves, but they widen your options. The practical question is what to spend the extra room on. In our experience the best returns come from small, boring improvements: customers who can find you online, orders that no longer get retyped, and reports that show you what is working.

## A sensible first step

Do not start with the biggest idea. Pick the one task your team repeats the most, and ask whether a computer could do part of it. That is exactly how most of our pilots begin: one site, one problem, a few weeks of real data.

Budget measures stay proposals until Parliament passes the Finance Bill and the agencies publish their terms, so check the details of any fund before you plan around it.

# Usability test script (5 SME owners)

> From audit 10.6. The cheapest, most valuable test: 5 people, about 20 minutes each, "it costs nothing but kopi". Five users typically reveal most of the major problems.

## Who
5 SME owners: friends, relatives or past clients. At least 3 should test on **their own phone** (a mid-range Android on 4G is ideal).

## How
- Open wssdevlabs.com in front of them. Say: "I'm testing the website, not you. Think out loud."
- **Don't help.** If they're stuck for 30 seconds, note it and move on.
- Write down every hesitation, wrong tap and "huh?".

## Tasks
1. "In 10 seconds, tell me what this company does." (Hide the screen after 10 seconds.)
2. "How much would a website for your business cost?"
3. "Find a system for managing visitors to your office. What would it cost, and what do you get?"
4. "How do you contact them? What happens after that?"
5. "Would you trust them with RM3,000? Why or why not?"

## After
Fix the problems that 2 or more people hit, first. **If they can't do task 1 in 10 seconds, the homepage isn't done.**

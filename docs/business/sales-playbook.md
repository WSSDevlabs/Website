# Sales playbook

> From audit 7.4, 12.8 and 1.6. Short on purpose: use it every time a lead comes in.

## The promise (say it the same way everywhere)
**"We reply within one business day. Fixed quote within three."** Keep it on your worst week.

## Where leads come from
- The contact form (Web3Forms, to your inbox). The `package` field tells you which button they came from (for example `business`, `wss-pos`, `founding`, `smart-premises`).
- The short form on the home page (email or WhatsApp number only).
- WhatsApp (mobile header icon, footer, "Buy now" and "Book a walkthrough" on system pages, "Book a site visit" on complete solutions, the website quote calculator).
- The Botpress chat assistant (check its conversations every day).
- Email.

## WhatsApp Business setup
- One WhatsApp Business number: +6011-2720-5895.
- **Labels:** New · Quoted · Won · Lost · Founding Client.
- **Quick replies** for: prices (link to /websites/ or /systems/), what you need to prepare, payment terms, hosting after year 1, "can I see it working?".
- **Catalog:** the 15 public systems, with the same names and prices as the site.

## CRM sheet (Google Sheets or Notion)
Columns: Date · Name · Business · Source (form / WhatsApp / referral / GBP) · Need (Website / System / Custom / Ecosystem) · Budget · Quote no. · Quote amount · Care Plan in quote (Y/N) · Status · Next follow-up date · Notes.

## From first message to signed quote
1. **Reply within one business day.** Thank them, ask 2 or 3 questions, offer a 15-minute call.
2. **15-minute call.** Ask about budget, deadline and what success looks like here, not in the form.
3. **Fixed quote within three business days**, using `docs/legal/statement-of-work-template.md`, with a Care Plan pre-selected (they can untick it).
4. **Follow up on day 2, 7 and 14.** Most small studios lose deals by not following up.
5. On acceptance: deposit invoice, kickoff date, content checklist.

## How to present the price (audit 12.8)
1. **Outcome first, then price:** "Get enquiries on WhatsApp from Google: RM2,990."
2. **Anchor honestly:** "Malaysian agencies typically quote RM3,000 to 8,000 for a custom SME site; ours is RM2,990 because we're a lean, founder-led studio." Keep the sources handy (docs/business/claims-evidence.md).
3. **Systems:** show the own vs rent maths, both sides, with the calculator on /systems/.
4. **Pre-select a Care Plan.**
5. **Founding Client offer** for the first 5: "I'm building my case study library." Use the Founding Client Agreement. Update the counter in the CMS.
6. **Never discount silently.** If they push, remove scope (fewer pages, no blog), not price.

## System orders (until a real checkout exists)
1. "Buy now" on a system page opens WhatsApp with "I'd like to buy [system] ([price]). Please send the invoice."
2. Within one business day: confirm any extras (installation, branding, training, data import), then send the invoice (bank transfer or FPX link) with links to the Licence Agreement (/licence/) and Refund Policy (/refunds/), and ask them to reply "I accept the licence and refund terms". Keep that reply: it's your record that they accepted.
3. When payment clears: email the download link, licence and install guide. Keep a copy of the email as the delivery record.
4. If installation was added: schedule it within 3 working days of hosting access.
5. Start the 30-day support and 12-month update clocks from delivery.

## Partnerships (audit 7.1)
Accountants, company secretaries and e-invoicing consultants who serve SMEs. Offer 10% referral on the first project. Put it in writing.

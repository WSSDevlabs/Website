# SME Tier Launch Campaign — WSS Devlabs
**Prepared by:** /cmo · **Date:** 2026-08-09 · **Status:** Ready to run, pending real business details (see Admin note at bottom)

## Positioning
WSS Devlabs now serves two audiences from one team: growing companies who need
custom software (existing Launch/Build/Enterprise tiers), and Malaysian SMEs /
traditional businesses who just need to get online, fast and cheap (new
Starter/Business tiers). This campaign targets the second audience exclusively —
don't blend this messaging with the "full digital solutions studio" positioning
used for the growth tier.

## Target audience
Hawkers, kedai runcit, bengkel (workshops), small F&B outlets, home-based
businesses, and traditional service traders (tailors, contractors, salons) in
Malaysia who:
- Have no website, or only a Facebook/Instagram page
- Are price-sensitive and skeptical that "a website" is worth the cost
- May be eligible for the MSME Digital Grant MADANI (registered ≥6 months, turnover
  ≥RM50,000) — this is the wedge that gets a "too expensive" objection to flip

## Core message (use consistently across channels)
**"Get your business online from RM500 — and the government may pay half."**

Never say "free" or imply full grant coverage. The grant is a 50% match up to
RM5,000; state it as "we help you claim it," not "it's covered."

## What NOT to do
- Don't claim the grant makes the website "free" — it's a 50% match, capped at
  RM5,000, first-come-first-served, one claim per business (see
  `.claude/skills/admin/templates/grant-assistance-info-sheet.md`).
- Don't run this messaging on pages/audiences meant for the growth tier
  (Launch/Build/Enterprise) — it will undercut the premium positioning there.
- Don't post fabricated testimonials or case studies to make the SME tier look
  more proven than it is — the site's own content gate policy (README.md) exists
  for exactly this reason.

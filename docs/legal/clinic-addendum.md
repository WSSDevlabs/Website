## 1. Intended purpose
The Clinic Management system is **administrative and record-keeping software**: appointments, registration, queue, visit notes, billing and reminders. It is **not intended for diagnosis, treatment, monitoring of patients, or interpreting results**, and it does not calculate doses or suggest diagnoses.

WSS will not add any feature with a clinical purpose without a regulatory review first, because such software can be a regulated medical device requiring registration with the Medical Device Authority.

## 2. Sensitive data
- The Clinic is the data controller and obtains **explicit consent** for health information at registration, using consent wording the Clinic approves.
- Role-based access: front-desk staff can't see clinical notes.
- Access logs are kept for [period].
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily, and hosted in Malaysia: [host].
- WSS acts as data processor under the Data Processing Agreement.

## 3. Medical records
- The Clinic sets its own record retention policy, following its obligations under private healthcare facilities law. The system supports retention settings and export.

## 4. AI WhatsApp assistant
- Admin topics only: booking, rescheduling, hours, directions, prices.
- Medical questions are handed to staff with a fixed message, for example: "I can't answer medical questions. A member of our team will reply shortly."

## 5. Advertising
- The Clinic is responsible for its website and marketing content complying with medical advertising laws and professional guidelines. WSS may refuse to publish claims such as guaranteed results or before-and-after promises.

## 6. Care Pro
- Care Pro is required while the system is in use.

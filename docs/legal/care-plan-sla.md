## 3. Definitions

- **Working day:** Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm, excluding Malaysian public holidays.
- **Response time:** time until WSS starts work on a request and tells the client what happens next. Not a fix time.
- **Critical issue:** the site or system is down for all users, or there is a suspected security incident.
- **Small edits:** content changes (text, images, prices, a blog post, a menu item). Not new pages, features or redesigns.
- **Backups:** daily (weekly on Hosting Only), kept for at least 14 days, restored on request.

## 4. How to raise a request
WhatsApp +6011-2720-5895 or email info@wssdevlabs.com. For critical issues, call or WhatsApp and write "CRITICAL" at the start.

## 5. Not included
New features, pages or redesigns (quoted as Change Requests); problems caused by changes made by the client or others; third-party outages; hardware faults; malware cleanup caused by client-installed software (quoted); content writing beyond small edits.

## 6. Client responsibilities
Keep access and contact details current; tell WSS before making own changes; keep any self-managed plugins or integrations licensed.

## 7. Data protection
WSS acts as the client's data processor under the Data Processing Agreement, which must be signed before the plan starts.

## 8. Term, price changes and cancellation
- Monthly plans roll monthly; cancel any time, effective at the end of the paid month.
- Yearly plans: cancellation within 30 days of payment refunds the unused months; after that the plan runs to the end of the paid year.
- WSS gives at least 30 days' notice of price changes.
- Ecosystem packages require Care Pro while in use.

## 9. On cancellation
WSS hands over the latest backup, all credentials, and helps move hosting on request. WSS's access is removed and any client data returned or deleted under the DPA.

## 10. Liability
WSS's total liability under this SLA is limited to the fees paid in the previous 12 months. See the Terms or MSA for the full clause.

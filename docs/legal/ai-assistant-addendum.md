## 1. What the assistant does
Answers questions about the Client's business (prices, hours, services, location, bookings), captures enquiry details, and hands the conversation to a person when needed.

## 2. Disclosure to customers
The assistant always tells customers they are chatting with an automated assistant, for example:
> "You're chatting with [Business]'s automated assistant. Type 'staff' to reach a person."

A customer can reach a person at any time.

## 3. Knowledge base
- The Client approves the knowledge base (price list, FAQ, policies) before launch and after every change.
- The Client tells WSS when prices or policies change.

## 4. Limits
- The assistant must not give medical, legal or financial advice, and hands those questions to staff.
- AI answers can be wrong. WSS tunes the answers during setup and monthly, but does not guarantee accuracy.
- The Client is responsible for the business content the assistant gives, once approved.

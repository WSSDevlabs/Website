# Freelancer / Contractor Agreement

> **DRAFT FOR LAWYER REVIEW.** Must be signed **before** anyone outside WSS touches client material. Without a written assignment, the freelancer may own what they create, and WSS can't pass ownership to the client (Copyright Act 1987, verify). Don't use this for people who are really employees; that needs an employment contract and EPF, SOCSO and EIS.

**Between:** [Registered name of WSS DevLabs] ("**WSS**") and [Freelancer name / company, IC or registration no.] ("**Contractor**").
**Date:** [ ]

## 1. Work
1.1 The Contractor provides the services described in each written brief from WSS, for the agreed fee and deadline.
1.2 The Contractor works independently, using their own equipment, and is responsible for their own taxes.
1.3 The Contractor may not subcontract any work without WSS's written approval.

## 2. Intellectual property
2.1 The Contractor **assigns to WSS** all copyright and other rights in everything created for WSS under this agreement, on creation. This assignment is in writing and signed, as required to be effective.
2.2 The Contractor waives moral rights to the extent the law allows.
2.3 The Contractor will not include third-party or open-source material without telling WSS and listing its licence. No GPL/AGPL code in ready-made systems without written approval.
2.4 The Contractor's own pre-existing tools stay theirs; the Contractor grants WSS and its clients a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any that are included in the work.

## 3. Confidentiality
3.1 The Contractor keeps all WSS and client information confidential, during and after the engagement, and uses it only for the work.
3.2 No client material is pasted into AI tools unless WSS has confirmed in writing that the tool's terms allow it.

## 4. Personal data (PDPA)
4.1 Where the Contractor handles personal data, they act as WSS's sub-processor: only on WSS's instructions, kept secure, never transferred elsewhere, and reported to WSS immediately if a breach happens.

## 5. Access
5.1 The Contractor gets the minimum access needed. All access is removed at the end of each brief.

## 6. End of engagement
6.1 On request or at the end, the Contractor returns or deletes all WSS and client material and confirms in writing.

## 7. Non-solicitation
7.1 For 12 months after the last brief, the Contractor will not directly offer services to WSS clients they were introduced to through WSS, without WSS's written consent.

## 8. Fees and payment
8.1 Fees per brief, invoiced on delivery, paid within [14] days of acceptance.

## 9. Law
9.1 Malaysian law applies.

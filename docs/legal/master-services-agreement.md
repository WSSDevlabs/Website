# Master Services Agreement (MSA)

> **DRAFT FOR LAWYER REVIEW.** Plain-English template from audit 14.5. Not legal advice. Use for custom builds, ecosystems, and any project over about RM5,000 or involving personal data or hardware.

**This agreement is between:**

- **[Registered name of WSS DevLabs]** (SSM [number]), of [registered address] ("**WSS**", "we"); and
- **[Client registered name]** ([registration number]), of [address] ("**Client**", "you").

**Date:** [date]

## 1. How this agreement works

1.1 This MSA holds the legal terms. Each project is described in a **Statement of Work** (SOW) that we both sign. Schedules (Care Plan SLA, Data Processing Agreement, Hardware Schedule, Clinic Addendum, AI Assistant Addendum) apply when a SOW says so.

1.2 **Order of precedence:** SOW, then schedules, then this MSA, except that the protections in clauses 9 (liability), 10 (indemnity) and 12 (confidentiality) of this MSA always apply.

## 2. Scope

2.1 We deliver the items listed in the SOW. Anything not listed is out of scope and needs a Change Request.

2.2 Each SOW states: who it's for, scope, exclusions, revisions, timeline, what you provide, acceptance criteria, warranty, payment and support, plus any assumptions (for example "content provided within 10 working days").

## 3. Client inputs

3.1 You provide the content, information, decisions and access listed in the SOW within the agreed time. Delays on your side move the timeline.

3.2 You name one decision-maker who replies within 2 working days.

3.3 If we don't receive what we need for 30 days, we may pause the project, invoice the work completed, and reschedule when you're ready.

## 4. Change control

4.1 Changes outside scope are quoted in writing, at a fixed price with the effect on the timeline, before work starts. We only start once you approve the Change Request in writing.

## 5. Acceptance

5.1 When we deliver a milestone, you test it and either accept it or list the specific defects within **5 working days**.

5.2 If you don't respond within that time, or you start using the deliverable live, the milestone is **deemed accepted**.

5.3 We fix listed defects that fall within the agreed scope, then the milestone is accepted.

## 6. Fees and payment

6.1 Fees and the payment schedule are in the SOW. Standard schedules: custom builds 30% to start, 40% at the staging demo, 30% at handover; websites 50/50; stores 40/30/30.

6.2 Invoices are due within **7 days**. Overdue amounts carry interest at **[x]% per year** (verify a reasonable rate; section 75 of the Contracts Act 1950 only allows reasonable compensation, not penalties).

6.3 If a payment is more than 14 days late, we may suspend work and support after written notice.

6.4 Prices are final. No SST is added while WSS is not SST-registered (verify with your accountant before each contract).

## 7. Intellectual property

7.1 **Project deliverables:** on full payment, you own the deliverables created specifically for you under the SOW.

7.2 **Background IP:** we keep ownership of our pre-existing and general tools, templates, code libraries, the ready-made system cores and know-how ("Background IP"). We give you a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use the Background IP included in your deliverables, for your own business.

7.3 **Third-party and open-source components** are licensed under their own terms, listed in a NOTICE file at handover.

7.4 Until full payment, we may withhold source code and credentials.

## 8. Warranty

8.1 For **[30/60] days** after acceptance (60 for custom builds), we fix defects in the work we built that stop it from working as described in the SOW.

8.2 The warranty does not cover changes made by others, third-party services, hardware, hosting you manage, or misuse. We don't promise the software will be error-free or uninterrupted. We never guarantee search rankings.

## 9. Limitation of liability

9.1 Our total liability under each SOW is limited to the fees you paid under that SOW. For Care Plans, it is limited to the fees paid in the previous 12 months.

9.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses, including lost profits, revenue, goodwill, or data beyond restoring from our latest backup.

9.3 Nothing limits liability for fraud, or anything that cannot be limited by law.

## 10. Client responsibilities and indemnity

10.1 You are responsible for the content and data you provide, and for having the rights and consents to use them (including consents under the PDPA).

10.2 You will compensate us for claims caused by your content, your data collection, or your use of the deliverables in breach of law.

## 11. Data protection

11.1 Where we process personal data on your behalf, you are the data controller and we are the data processor. We process it only on your instructions and under the Data Processing Agreement.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each party keeps the other's confidential information private and uses it only for the engagement, during the engagement and for **3 years** afterwards. Exceptions: information that is public, already known, independently developed, or that must be disclosed by law.

## 13. Third-party services

13.1 Some features rely on third parties (hosting, payment gateways, WhatsApp, AI providers, SMS, maps). Their availability, prices and terms are outside our control. Their usage fees are paid by you, at cost, unless the SOW says otherwise.

## 14. Non-solicitation

14.1 During the engagement and for 12 months afterwards, neither party will directly hire the other's staff or contractors who worked on the project without written consent.

## 15. Termination

15.1 Either party may end this agreement if the other materially breaches it and doesn't fix the breach within 14 days of written notice.

15.2 On termination you pay for work done up to that date, and we hand over completed and paid deliverables, credentials and data.

## 16. Force majeure

16.1 Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control (natural disasters, outages of major providers, government action), provided it notifies the other promptly.

## 17. Complaints and disputes

17.1 Complaints: we acknowledge within 2 working days and respond within 10 working days.

17.2 Malaysian law applies. The parties first try good-faith negotiation, then mediation (for example through the Asian International Arbitration Centre), then the courts of Kuala Lumpur. For contracts above RM[amount], either party may instead refer the dispute to AIAC arbitration.

## 18. General

18.1 This MSA, the SOWs and schedules are the whole agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Notices go to the addresses above (email is fine).

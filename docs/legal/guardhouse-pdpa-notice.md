**[Name of management body / company]** collects your name, phone number, [vehicle plate], [photo] and [the last 4 digits of your IC] when you register as a visitor.

**Why:** to keep residents, staff and the building safe, and to know who is on the premises.
**Who sees it:** authorised management and security staff, and our system provider acting on our instructions.
**How long:** [90] days, then deleted, unless needed for an incident investigation.

**Your rights:** you can ask to see or correct your data, or ask questions: [contact person, phone, email].

Providing these details is required to enter. If you don't want to provide them, please speak to the guard.

## 2. What the Client provides in return (within 30 days of launch)
1. A case study interview (about 30 minutes) about the problem, what was built and the results, with real numbers where possible.
2. Written permission to publish the Client's business name, logo and approved screenshots on wssdevlabs.com and WSS DevLabs social media.
3. An **honest** public review on Google. The discount does not depend on the review being positive.
4. A 15-minute feedback call.

## 3. Approval
- WSS sends the case study text and screenshots to the Client before publishing. The Client may correct facts and ask for confidential details to be removed.
- Screenshots use sample data, or personal data is blurred.
- The Client may ask WSS to remove the case study later; WSS removes it within 14 days.

## 4. If the items in section 2 aren't provided
If the case study, permission and review are not provided within 30 days of launch (or a later date agreed in writing), the discount amount in section 1 becomes payable, invoiced with 14 days to pay.

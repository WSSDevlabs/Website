## Rates — pricing ladder (set 2026-08-09)

**Market context (research 2026-08-09):** Malaysia freelancer project pricing runs
RM1,500–RM6,000; standard agency SME sites (5–10pg) run RM3,000–RM10,000; small
studios RM3,000–RM10,000; full agencies RM8,000–RM25,000+. Founder directive: enter
~75% below the low end of that (~RM2,000 reference point) to win on quantity.

| Tier | Segment | Price | Scope | Turnaround |
|------|---------|-------|-------|------------|
| Starter | SME / traditional business | From RM 500 | 1-page, up to 4 sections, 1 revision | 5 business days |
| Business | SME / traditional business | From RM 1,500 | Up to 5 pages, 2 revisions, GBP setup | 7–10 business days |
| Launch | Growth | From RM 3,000 | Up to 8 pages, custom-designed | 2–4 weeks |
| Build | Growth | From RM 15,000 | App / custom CMS / AI feature | 6–10 weeks |
| Enterprise | Growth | Custom quote | ERP / bespoke architecture | scoped individually |
| Care Plan (add-on, any tier) | Recurring | RM 99–199 / month | Hosting, updates, up to 2 minor edits/mo | ongoing |

**Sustainability rule:** Starter/Business are volume/lead-gen tiers — template-based,
capped revisions. They only stay profitable at RM500–1,500 if scope stays this tight;
do not add custom design or unlimited revisions to these tiers without repricing.
The Care Plan is the recurring-revenue engine that keeps the low one-time prices
sustainable for a solo founder.

**Grant angle:** Starter/Business are positioned as compatible with the MSME Digital
Grant MADANI — **50% matching, up to RM5,000, not full coverage** (client still pays
the other half). Administered by MDEC, first-come-first-served against a fixed
RM50M pool, one claim per business, requires ≥6 months SSM registration and ≥RM50,000
annual turnover. Budget 2026 separately allocated RM150M for SME digitalisation
grants nationally. Never market this as "free" — see the corrected talking points in
`.claude/skills/admin/templates/grant-assistance-info-sheet.md` (updated 2026-08-09
after cross-checking beyond the initial research pass).

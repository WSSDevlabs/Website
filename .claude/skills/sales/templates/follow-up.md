# Follow-up templates

Keep them short. One clear question per message. Use the lead's language.

## 1. After first contact (day 2)

**EN**
Hi [name], thanks for reaching out about [need]. To point you in the right direction,
could we do a 20-minute call this week? [Day] or [day], [time], work for you?

**BM**
Hai [nama], terima kasih kerana menghubungi kami tentang [keperluan]. Boleh kita buat
panggilan 20 minit minggu ini? [Hari] atau [hari], pukul [masa], sesuai?

## 2. No reply (day 5–7)

**EN**
Hi [name], just checking in on [need]. Is this still something you're looking at,
or has the timing changed?

**BM**
Hai [nama], saya ingin bertanya tentang [keperluan]. Adakah ini masih dalam
perancangan, atau masanya sudah berubah?

## 3. After the proposal (day 3 after sending)

**EN**
Hi [name], did you get a chance to look at the proposal? I'm happy to walk through it
or adjust the scope to fit your budget.

**BM**
Hai [nama], sudahkah sempat melihat cadangan yang kami hantar? Saya boleh terangkan
atau ubah skop supaya sesuai dengan bajet anda.

## 4. Last follow-up (close the loop)

**EN**
Hi [name], I haven't heard back, so I'll close this on my side for now. If [need]
comes back on the table, just reply to this message.

**BM**
Hai [nama], memandangkan belum ada maklum balas, saya akan tutup perkara ini buat
masa sekarang. Jika [keperluan] kembali diperlukan, balas sahaja mesej ini.

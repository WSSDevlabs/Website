# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement
*Draft — review before signing*

**Date:** [date]

**Between:**
1. **WSS Devlabs** ([SSM registration no.]), [address] ("WSS Devlabs"); and
2. **[Client legal name]** ([SSM registration no.]), [address] ("Client"),

each a "Party", together the "Parties".

## 1. Purpose
The Parties want to discuss [a potential software project / the project named
"[name]"] (the "Purpose") and may share confidential information to do so.

## 2. Confidential information
Any non-public information one Party (the "Discloser") shares with the other (the
"Recipient") in connection with the Purpose, in any form: business plans, customer
data, source code, designs, pricing, credentials and technical documentation.

It does not include information that the Recipient can show:
(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient;
(b) the Recipient already knew without a duty of confidence;
(c) the Recipient receives lawfully from a third party without restriction; or
(d) the Recipient develops independently without using the Discloser's information.

## 3. Obligations
The Recipient will:
(a) use confidential information only for the Purpose;
(b) protect it with at least reasonable care, and no less care than it uses for its
    own confidential information;
(c) share it only with staff or contractors who need it for the Purpose and are bound
    by confidentiality duties at least as strict as these; and
(d) promptly tell the Discloser of any unauthorised disclosure it becomes aware of.

## 4. Required disclosure
The Recipient may disclose confidential information if required by law or a court or
regulator, after giving the Discloser prompt written notice where lawful.

## 5. Personal data
Where confidential information includes personal data, the Recipient will handle it in
line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia) as amended.

## 6. Return or destruction
On written request, or when discussions end, the Recipient will return or destroy the
confidential information, except copies kept in routine backups or required by law,
which stay subject to this agreement.

## 7. Term
This agreement starts on the date above. Obligations last **[2] years** from the last
disclosure, and indefinitely for source code, credentials and personal data.

## 8. No licence, no obligation
No licence or ownership right passes under this agreement. Neither Party is obliged to
enter into any further agreement.

## 9. Governing law
Laws of Malaysia. The courts of [Kuala Lumpur] have jurisdiction.

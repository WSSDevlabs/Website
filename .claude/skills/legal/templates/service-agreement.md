# Master Service Agreement
*Draft — review before signing*

**Date:** [date] · **Agreement #:** WSS-MSA-[YYYY]-[NNN]

**Between:** **WSS Devlabs** ([SSM no.]), [address] ("Provider") and
**[Client legal name]** ([SSM no.]), [address] ("Client").

## 1. Structure
This agreement sets the terms for all work the Provider does for the Client. Each
piece of work is described in a Statement of Work ("SOW") that references this
agreement. If an SOW conflicts with this agreement, the SOW wins for that work only.

## 2. Services
The Provider delivers the deliverables in each SOW, meeting the acceptance criteria
listed there.

## 3. Client responsibilities
The Client provides the access, content, feedback and decisions an SOW lists, by the
dates given. Delays on the Client side move the timeline day for day and are not a
breach by the Provider.

## 4. Fees and payment
(a) Fees and the payment schedule are set in each SOW and its quotation.
(b) Invoices are due **[14] days** from the invoice date.
(c) Prices exclude Service Tax (SST), which is added where the Provider is registered.
(d) If an invoice is unpaid **[14] days** after its due date, the Provider may pause
    work until it is paid. The timeline moves by the length of the pause.

## 5. Acceptance
The Client has **[5] business days** after delivery to accept a deliverable or list
specific failures against the SOW's acceptance criteria. If the Client sends nothing
in that time, the deliverable is accepted. Fixing listed failures restarts the window
for those items only.

## 6. Change requests
Work outside an SOW's deliverables needs a written change request with its own price
and timeline, approved in writing (email is enough) before it starts.

## 7. Intellectual property
(a) On **full payment** of an SOW, ownership of the custom deliverables made for that
    SOW passes to the Client.
(b) Until full payment, the Provider grants the Client a licence to use the
    deliverables for evaluation only.
(c) The Provider keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, libraries, templates and
    know-how, and grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use any of
    them that are built into the deliverables.
(d) Open-source components stay under their own licences.

## 8. Confidentiality
Each Party keeps the other's confidential information confidential, on the terms of
the Parties' NDA dated [date] or, if none, on terms no less protective than reasonable
industry practice.

## 9. Personal data
Where the Provider processes personal data for the Client, the Provider acts as the
Client's **data processor** under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia) as
amended. The Provider will:
(a) process personal data only on the Client's documented instructions;
(b) keep it secure with appropriate technical and organisational measures;
(c) notify the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data
    breach affecting the Client's data; and
(d) return or delete the data at the end of the engagement, unless the law requires
    keeping it.

## 10. Warranty
For **[30] days** after acceptance, the Provider fixes, at no charge, defects where a
deliverable does not meet its acceptance criteria. This warranty excludes problems
caused by changes made by others, third-party services or hosting, and misuse. Work
after this period falls under a support retainer or a new SOW.

## 11. Limitation of liability
(a) Each Party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the
    Client paid under the SOW the claim relates to, in the 12 months before the claim.
(b) Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost
    profit, revenue or data.
(c) This clause does not limit liability for fraud, or for anything the law does not
    allow to be limited.

## 12. Term and termination
(a) This agreement continues until ended by either Party with **[30] days'** written
    notice.
(b) Either Party may end it immediately if the other materially breaches it and does
    not fix the breach within [14] days of written notice.
(c) On termination, the Client pays for work done up to the termination date.
    Clause 7 then applies to the work that has been paid for.

## 13. General
Laws of Malaysia apply. The courts of [Kuala Lumpur] have jurisdiction. Neither Party
may transfer this agreement without the other's written consent. This agreement, its
SOWs and quotations are the whole agreement on their subject.

# Using the MSME Digital Grant MADANI for Your Website — Info Sheet

For clients considering the Starter or Business package, share this before the quote
call — it often turns "RM 500/1,500 is still too much" into "let's start."

## What the grant is
The MSME Digital Grant MADANI (MDEC-administered) matches **50% of eligible digital
spending, up to RM5,000** — the client still pays the other half themselves. It is
**not** a full-cost cover. It's disbursed first-come, first-served against a fixed
RM50 million pool (~100,000 SMEs targeted), and each business can only claim it
once. Budget 2026 also added a separate Digital Acceleration Grant (RM53 million,
AI/blockchain/cybersecurity) for larger digital projects — not typically relevant to
Starter/Business website clients.

## Who qualifies (typical criteria — verify against the current official terms before quoting)
- Malaysian-registered business, at least 60% Malaysian-owned
- Valid SSM (or Local Authority) registration, **operating at least 6 months**
- Annual turnover of **at least RM50,000** (not just a ceiling — brand-new
  businesses under this threshold may not yet qualify)
- Must not have received a previous digitalisation matching grant
- Tax filings up to date

## What this means for our pricing (be precise with clients)
- Starter (RM 500): grant covers up to RM250 → client pays as little as ~RM250
- Business (RM 1,500): grant covers up to RM750 → client pays as little as ~RM750
- Never say "free" or "near-zero cost" — it's a 50% match, not full coverage.

## What we provide the client
- A formal invoice matching the grant's documentation requirements
- A written scope description (from the SOW) they can attach to their application
- Confirmation letter of services rendered, on request, at handover

## What we don't do
We do not file the grant application on the client's behalf, and we do not
guarantee approval or fund availability (it's first-come, first-served against a
capped pool) — that's between the client and MDEC. We help them have clean
paperwork to apply with.

## Talking points for the quote call
- "This package is affordable on its own — and if you qualify for the MADANI grant,
  the government matches half of it, up to RM5,000."
- "We'll give you everything you need to apply — you just submit, and funds are
  first-come, first-served, so it's worth checking early."
- If the business is brand new (under 6 months) or turnover is still under RM50,000:
  "You may not qualify yet — worth starting with us now and applying once you cross
  that mark."
